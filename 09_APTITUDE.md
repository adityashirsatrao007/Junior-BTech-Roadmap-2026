# 09 — Aptitude (crack the mass-recruiter round)

Mass recruiters (TCS/Infosys/Wipro/Accenture/Capgemini/IBM) hire heavily on aptitude + basic English. You'll practice **30 min/day** in the 9:00–9:30 slot. This alone can decide your first offer.

---

## 1. What they actually test

| Block | Topics | Weight |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Quantitative Aptitude** | Percentages, Profit/Loss, Time & Work, Speed/Distance, Ratios, Simple/Compound Interest, Number System, Permutations/Combinations, Probability, Averages | high |
| **Logical Reasoning** | Blood relations, Directions, Series, Coding-decoding, Puzzles, Syllogisms | high |
| **Verbal** | Error spotting, Synonyms/Antonyms, Reading comprehension, Fill in the blanks | medium |
| **Data Interpretation** | Tables, Bar/Pie charts, ratios from graphs | medium |

Most companies set a **cutoff** (e.g., score ≥70%) — you don't need to be first, you need to clear it *consistently*.

---

## 2. Plan (30 min/day, every day)

**Weeks 1–2:** pick ONE topic/week. Start with Percentage → then Profit&Loss (they chain).
**Every day:** 15 min theory + 15 min timed MCQs (aim <2 min/question).
**Saturday slot:** 30-min full mock (45 Qs), log the score.
**Monthly:** 2 company-style mocks (Unstop has free ones).

Cycle order (6 weeks then repeat faster):
`Percentage → Profit/Loss → Time & Work → Speed Distance → Number System → Ratios/Mixtures → SI/CI → P&C → Probability → Data Interpretation → Reasoning mix`

## 4. Shortcuts to train (they pay off)

- **Percentage:** `10% = /10`, `5% = half of that`, `1% = /100` — split to compute fast.
- **Time & Work:** if A does job in `a` days and B in `b`, together = `ab/(a+b)` days.
- **Speed/Distance:** convert km/h → m/s by `× 5/18`.
- **Profit/Loss:** SP = CP × (1 ± p%). Always solve with CP = 100 trick.
- **Averages:** weighted average = total/total-weights.
- **Reasoning:** draw diagrams for directions/blood relations — never solve in your head.

**The rule:** if you can't explain the shortcut, your base is weak → redo the underlying topic.

---

## DO / DON'T

**DO** — time yourself daily; maintain an error log (which topics you keep failing); attempt mocks on Sundays.
**DON'T** — practice random topics daily (follow the cycle); skip data interpretation (beginners fear it, it's easy marks); leave the verbal block for "later".
